    ·Hello!  I'm  Alex  Marlowe·

    Creative developer & designer, based in Oslo, Norway. I build expressive websites, brand systems and moving images — always starting from the same question: what should this make people feel?

    My practice sits where design systems meet storytelling: from information architecture and UX flows to typography, motion and full front-end builds. One idea, translated coherently across every medium it touches.

    Available for freelance — 2026

    Right now I take a small number of independent projects, helping teams ship interfaces that are clear, fast and a little strange — in the good way.

    "Styles come and go.
    Good design is a language,
    not a style."

    — Massimo Vignelli
    10+ years · 60+ projects · Oslo → Worldwide

    Work should age like good code: readable, honest and easy to refactor. I test everything on real screens, real thumbs and real bad connections — delight that only works on a demo laptop is not delight.

    This entire site is hand-built with HTML · CSS · JS. Every image in the archive is generated from a seed — the whole archive is code.

    ··Mastering·· Your Brand's Signal
    [ · d n a · ]

    ·Let's find  what sets  your brand  apart·

    Every project starts with listening. Before grids and gradients there is a conversation — what you make, who it serves, and why anyone should care. That answer becomes the signal; everything else is amplification.

    From there I design the language your signal speaks: identity, interface, photography, motion. Coherent on a billboard, on a phone, or inside a terminal window. Let's work together — the goal is work that does not just look right, but reads unmistakably as yours.
